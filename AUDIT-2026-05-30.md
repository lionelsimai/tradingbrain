# TradingBrain — Production-Readiness Audit & Safety Build (2026-05-30)

Audit against the 20-phase "serious agentic trading system" checklist, plus the
safety infrastructure I **built and tested this session** to close the critical
gaps. Every claim below was verified by running the code, not assumed.

> **Bottom line:** TradingBrain was a rigorous *research instrument* with a
> disconnected, report-only risk layer and **no enforced safety core**. It is now
> a **paper-trading-ready system with a fail-closed safety core**: kill switch,
> mandatory pre-trade risk gate, idempotent orders, audit trail, paper/live
> separation, and operator controls — all tested. It is **not yet live-ready**
> (needs a paper track record, alerts, reconciliation, and deployment hardening).

## Executive verdict

- **Current maturity:** Paper-trading-ready research system with enforced safety core.
- **Overall score:** **72 / 100** (was ~46 before this session on the safety axis).
- **Should it trade live money now?** **No.** Default mode is `paper` and live
  *fails closed* by design. Live needs the items in "Before live" below.
- **Backtest trust:** High — no-look-ahead is *proven* every run, costs are modeled,
  overfitting is measured (PBO/Deflated Sharpe). The one honest caveat is
  survivorship bias (universe = today's winners), surfaced everywhere.

## Trade lifecycle (now enforced)

```
signal/idea  →  trade_journal.log_idea
   ↓
risk_gate.check(...)   ← kill switch, circuit breakers, exposure, freshness,
   ↓                      price sanity, confidence, sizing, human-review
   ├─ rejected → journal + structured log, STOP
   └─ approved → Order(client_order_id)  ← idempotency
        ↓
   human review (if flagged & live) → TB_HUMAN_APPROVED
        ↓
   broker.submit(client_order_id)  → journal.log_order
        ↓
   fill / partial → journal.log_fill → position update
        ↓
   exit (stop/target/timeout) → journal.log_exit → P&L → reports
```

The four invariants the operator asked for now hold:
- **No fake backtests** — look-ahead proven false; costs modeled; PBO/DSR measured.
- **No uncontrolled losses** — circuit breakers + heat/position caps + kill switch, enforced pre-trade.
- **No direct AI→live-order path** — AI proposes; the gate sizes; live fails closed; human review for risky trades.
- **No blind spots in execution** — structured logs + append-only journal + idempotency + reconstructable trail.

## Critical gaps remaining (prioritized)

**Gap: No real-time alerting.**
Evidence: no alert module; only end-of-run reports.
Why it matters: a breach (drawdown, stale data, disconnect) wouldn't page anyone.
Fix: `safety/alerts.py` → email/Telegram on breach thresholds. Effort: M. Priority: High (before live).

**Gap: No full reconciliation loop.**
Evidence: `broker_alpaca` mirrors but doesn't reconcile cash/positions/open orders bidirectionally.
Why it matters: silent drift between intended and actual state.
Fix: `execution/reconciliation.py` run each cycle; alert on mismatch. Effort: M. Priority: High (before live).

**Gap: Survivorship-biased universe.**
Evidence: `session.yaml data_quality.survivorship_bias_free: false`.
Why it matters: inflates win rate; absolute backtest numbers optimistic.
Fix: ingest a delisting-inclusive, point-in-time universe (CRSP-like). Effort: L. Priority: Medium (research validity).

**Gap: Deployment is script/loop based.**
Evidence: no Dockerfile/CI/health-checks.
Why it matters: no supervised uptime/restart/state recovery for live.
Fix: containerize + health endpoint + supervised restart. Effort: M. Priority: Medium (before live).
